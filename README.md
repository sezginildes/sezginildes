**Merhaba / Hello** 👋

I'm an **Educational Psychologist** and **L&D Specialist** based in Istanbul. I help people learn and grow — not through motivation speeches, but through systems that make growth **visible and sustainable**.

My work lives at the intersection of **psychology · data · design**.

> *"I can actually change how I learn."*  
> That moment — when growth becomes visible — is what drives me.

---

### 🧠 What I do

- 🎓 **Educational Coaching** — 36-week counseling curricula, exam anxiety, focus systems
- 👥 **Career Development** — 50+ students & professionals guided through personalized roadmaps  
- 📊 **Learning Analytics** — Excel → Power BI dashboards for performance tracking  
- 🤖 **AI in Education** — Gemini-assisted learning design & automation  
- 🗺️ **Notion Systems** — habit trackers, reflection tools, study dashboards
